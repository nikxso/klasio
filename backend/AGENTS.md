# Project Guidelines & Notion Knowledge Base Rules

## Notion Research & Knowledge Rules

- **Knowledge Source**: Notion is the project's research/knowledge source.
- **Read Scope**: The agent may read from the attendance research page.
- **Proposals**: The agent may propose additions or corrections to the research.
- **Write Permissions**: The agent may write research findings, benchmark results, decisions, and open questions to Notion **only when explicitly authorized**.
- **Integrity & Overwrite Protections**:
  - Do NOT silently overwrite existing research.
  - Do NOT convert candidate technologies into selected technologies without evidence or an explicit decision.

## Research and Specification Workflow

For this project, the Notion page:

"Automated Student Attendance — Master Project Research & Technical Guide"

is the primary external research/knowledge source.

The Notion MCP server is available to this agent.

Before creating or modifying Spec Kit artifacts:
- consult the relevant Notion sections;
- distinguish research findings from candidate approaches;
- distinguish architectural/domain decisions from implementation choices;
- preserve unresolved alternatives;
- do not convert candidate technologies into requirements;
- do not invent requirements that are not supported by project discussions/research;
- do not silently rewrite or contradict existing Notion research.

When a new research result, benchmark, architectural decision, rejection,
or important unresolved question is discovered, propose updating Notion.
Only write to Notion when explicitly authorized.

Spec Kit artifacts should represent the current project specification and
decisions, while Notion remains the broader research/knowledge base.

Do not copy the entire Notion page into spec.md.
Read the relevant sections and synthesize only the information required
for the current specification.
